Meet Brian Blackmore

The person behind the party.

Brian has spent 15 years in live events, from running live sound and high-end production to planning events from the first idea to the last song. That background means the details most guests never notice, like clean sound, perfect cues and a timeline that runs on schedule, are handled before the night begins.

On the mic, Brian brings an energetic, personable MC style that reads the room and keeps it moving. He knows when to fill the dance floor, when to let a moment breathe, and how to make every announcement feel like part of the celebration.

Above all, he's passionate about people. He takes the time to understand what you're picturing, then executes on that vision so the event feels like yours, not a template.
